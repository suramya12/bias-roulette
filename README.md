# Bias Roulette

Spin a wheel of idols, a showdown picks one, spin a wheel of that idol's songs, a second showdown picks one, and the music video plays in the page. Static site, no dependencies, no build step.

## Two sides, one site

| Side | Where | What |
|---|---|---|
| **K-pop** | the site root (this README) | Bias Roulette of idols, K-pop Top 100 (Real 100 / Easy 100) |
| **Pop & Latin** | [`pop/`](pop/README.md) | Roulette of English and Latin artists (All / Favourites decks), Pop & Latin Top 100 |
| **Anime** | [`anime/`](anime/README.md) | Openings, endings and animated MVs. Data pipeline done; page coming. |

Both pages share one header row: the genre menu (K-pop / Pop & Latin), the Roulette and Top 100 tabs, and the Binder. Switching genre keeps you in the same mode. The two sides keep their own code, data, update scripts and binder. Nothing is shared but the URL, so either side can be rebuilt without touching the other.

## Run it

```bash
npm start
```

Open the address it prints. It also prints a `http://192.168...` address for your phone on the same Wi-Fi. Don't open `index.html` straight from disk: YouTube refuses players without a web address (player error 153).

```bash
npm test
```

runs the game-logic tests.

## Files

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `data/roster.json` | Groups, shared song sets and idols. Edit songs here. |
| `data/overrides.json` | Your manual video picks. Never written by scripts. |
| `data/videos.json`, `data/dropped.json` | Generated by the resolver. Don't edit. |
| `REVIEW.md`, `DROPPED.md` | Generated reports: every match, and what was left out. |
| `src/game.js` | Rules only (picking, scoring, phases). Tested. |
| `src/catalog.js` | Joins roster and videos. |
| `src/wheel.js`, `fx.js`, `sound.js`, `player.js`, `main.js` | Drawing, effects, audio, YouTube player, wiring. |
| `src/charts.js`, `data/charts.json` | Top 100 mode, and its data (built by `scripts/charts.mjs`). |
| `src/card.js`, `src/art.js` | Collectible cards, and the painter for their cover art. |
| `data/portraits.json` | Idol photos from Wikimedia Commons with credits (built by `scripts/portraits.mjs`). |
| `scripts/resolve.mjs`, `scripts/serve.mjs` | Video resolver, local server. |

## Changing songs or videos

1. Edit `data/roster.json` (add a title to a group's `songs`, a `sets` entry, or an idol's `solo`).
2. Run `npm run resolve`. It needs yt-dlp (`pip install yt-dlp`). Only new songs are searched.
3. Read `REVIEW.md`. Flagged rows are listed first.
4. To fix a match, add a line to `data/overrides.json` and run `npm run resolve` again:

```json
"IVE | LOVE DIVE": "https://youtu.be/Y8JFxS1HlDo",
"SOME ARTIST | Some Song": null
```

A link forces that video; `null` drops the song. Other options: `--refresh` (search everything again), `--retry-dropped`, `--recheck-flagged`, `--only "text"`.

The resolver rejects audio, lyric, visualizer, teaser, practice, live and broadcast uploads, prefers verified artist or label channels, and checks YouTube's embed page to see whether the video may play on other sites. Region locks can't be seen from one location; the player shows a "Watch on YouTube" link when a video fails.

## Autoplay

**Auto** (on the Roulette screen from the start) has two switches. **Autoplay**: when the MV ends, the next round is chosen and plays by itself (a video that can't play here is skipped after a few seconds). **Same idol**: keep the idol and draw another of their songs, never the one that just played; an idol with one song gets a new idol instead. Both are remembered in the browser.

Top 100 has its own **Auto** switch next to Pull: when an MV ends the ring does a quick 1.2 s spin and the next card plays (no stamp or confetti; a manual pull still gets the full show and works as Skip). In a background tab it skips the spin so the music keeps going.

Everywhere: a video that can't play here is skipped after 4 s; after 3 in a row Auto pauses and shows **Resume**. If the browser blocks autoplay, a **Tap to play** button appears.

## K-pop Top 100 mode

The toggle at the top switches to a second mode: a ring of photocards on a concert stage. Pull a card (button, tap the ring, or Enter/Space) and the ring spins round to it; tap again while it spins to stop it early. The winner glows in its tier colour as it comes round, and the celebration grows with rarity. Then its MV plays. The ring is `src/pull-machine.js`, shared with the Pop & Latin Top 100. Two lists:

- **Connoisseur**: the all-time Top 100, ranked by the average of Spotify streams (all versions of the song) and official music video views on YouTube. K-pop as a genre: groups, soloists, KPop Demon Hunters (HUNTR/X, Saja Boys) and K/DA.
- **New to K-pop**: 100 hand-picked entry points, ranked by the same score. Edit `NEWBIE` in `scripts/charts.mjs`.

`npm run charts` rebuilds `data/charts.json` from kworb.net (pages cached in `.cache/`; add `-- --fresh` for today's numbers). It prints songs it could not match, so you can add video ids to `EXTRA_VIDEOS`.

## Cards and collection

Every idol you win in Bias Roulette and every song you pull in the Top 100 becomes a collectible card (`src/card.js`). Rarity comes from the Top 100: Legendary (top 10), Epic (11-30), Rare (31-100), Common (not charted). Song cards show rank, Spotify streams and MV views. Idol cards show Power (how much their group and solo acts have charted), and how many group and solo songs are in their deck. Cards you collect are kept in the browser (localStorage) and shown in the collection binder.

Card art is painted in the browser by `src/art.js`, in a digital cel-shaded style. Song cards repaint a frame of the song's own MV. Idol cards repaint a freely licensed photo of that idol from Wikimedia Commons (the photographer and licence are credited under the big card). An idol with no usable photo gets a frame from an MV they lead, and no two idols ever share a picture. Each cover also gets a scene, a motif drawn from the song title (fire, moon, crown, disco ball...), and light effects that grow with rarity. `npm run portraits` refreshes `data/portraits.json`: it only accepts a Wikipedia article about that person (not their group) and an image hosted on Commons.

## Testing the player

`http://localhost:5173/?forceVideo=VIDEO_ID` plays that ID on every result. `IX1dkYoLHVs` (age-restricted) and `_k7gZaDjTTM` (embedding blocked) show the fallback.

## Deploy

No build: upload `index.html`, `styles.css`, `src/`, `data/` and the `pop/` folder (its `index.html`, `styles.css`, `src/`, `data/`) to any static host. All paths are relative, so a sub-path works. `.nojekyll` makes GitHub Pages serve the files as they are.

- **GitHub Pages**: push the folder to a repo, then Settings, Pages, Deploy from branch, `main`, `/ (root)`.
- **Netlify or Cloudflare Pages**: drag the folder into the dashboard, or connect the repo with no build command and the root as output folder.
